Bio

AVAN is an independent Irish folk singer-songwriter who blends his vocals and acoustic guitar to deliver songs and stories of love, heartbreak, hope and loneliness. Inspired by many artists and genres, his music bridges deeply personal storytelling with Irish folk traditions.

Following the successful release of his 2026 singles "It's All I Need" "Sarang My Dear," and "Hint of Regret," AVAN has played on stages across South Korea, Ireland and Germany with upcoming performances in the UK to round off the year.

AVAN has attracted an audience of over 60,000 followers from across the globe on all platforms in just a few months.

In 2027, AVAN will be hitting the UK and Europe festival circuit with more new releases on the way.
