Bio

AVAN is an independent folk singer-songwriter based in Dublin, blending acoustic guitar, bouzouki, and Irish tin whistle to create intimate, atmospheric soundscapes. With a background living and recording in South Korea, his music bridges deeply personal storytelling with transatlantic folk traditions.

Following the successful release of his 2026 singles "We're Strangers," "Sarang My Dear," and "Hint of Regret," AVAN is currently preparing for extensive touring across the UK and Europe in 2027.
